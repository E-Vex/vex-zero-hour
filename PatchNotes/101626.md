# AI Co-Pilot: an AI teammate for single player skirmish

This release introduces the **AI Co-Pilot**, an AI teammate that plays *with* you instead
of against you. At the start of a skirmish game the co-pilot automatically takes over the
opening for you: it runs the opening build sequence with your real dozers, keeps your base
rebuilt when it gets destroyed, keeps your factories producing and defends your base
perimeter while you focus on the fighting. You keep full manual control at all times - the
co-pilot never touches units you are actively commanding and attacking stays your decision.

**How to use it**

- Start any solo skirmish game. The co-pilot announces itself when the game begins:
  *"Co-Pilot online: I'll manage the base while you fight. Press F9 to change my orders."*
- Press **F9** at any time to cycle its level of control:
  1. **Opening** - runs the opening build order (supply, power, barracks, factory) and rebuilds those structures when destroyed.
  2. **Full** - opening plus continuous unit production, dozer/harvester upkeep and base perimeter defense.
  3. **Off** - the co-pilot detaches completely and you play fully manual.
- Save games keep the co-pilot and its current mode. Replays are not affected.

**Fair play**

The co-pilot only plays with information you already have:

- It only reacts to damage your own units and structures actually received - it never
  scans enemy positions through the fog of war.
- All buildings are placed with your own dozers at full cost and real build time.
- It only trains units you could train yourself through the same command sets.
- It never spends your last 300 dollars, and never takes over units you have ordered
  around (only completely idle units near your base get redirected to defend).
- Buildability rules for human players stay in force - no AI-only units or structures.

**Where it lives (for developers)**

The co-pilot is `AITeammatePlayer` (GameLogic/AI/AICoPilot.cpp), an `AIPlayer` subclass
attached to the local human player through `Player::attachAITeammate()`. It reuses the
engine's own dozer-construction machinery (`AIPlayer::doBaseBuilding`) so base building
and rebuilding behaves exactly like the built-in AI's construction. The F9 hotkey is
routed as a proper command message (`MSG_AI_COPILOT_TOGGLE`) from the client translator
(CommandXlat) to the logic thread (GameLogicDispatch), and auto-activation happens in
`GameLogic::tryStartNewGame` for fresh skirmish games only.

Tuning values (defense radius, money reserve, gatherer ratio, ...) are constants at the
top of `AICoPilot.h` and are deliberately conservative so the co-pilot helps you without
starving your economy.
